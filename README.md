# Simon van Lierde

I'm a PhD researcher and research software engineer at CML, the Institute of Environmental Sciences at Leiden University. I write open software and collect open data for industrial ecology and the circular economy.

- 🌐 Projects and CV: [simonvanlierde.github.io](https://simonvanlierde.github.io)
- 🔧 Main project: [R9lab](https://github.com/CMLPlatform/r9lab), where we record what products are made of by taking them apart ([app.r9lab.io](https://app.r9lab.io))
- 🆔 ORCID: [0009-0006-6953-909X](https://orcid.org/0009-0006-6953-909X)

Right now I'm getting the R9lab teardown data ready for its first citable release, fixing and benchmarking the LCA and input-output libraries I use (Brightway, pymrio, flodym), and keeping a few small tools going, like [jsonvet](https://github.com/simonvanlierde/jsonvet) and [credit-matrix](https://github.com/simonvanlierde/credit-matrix).
